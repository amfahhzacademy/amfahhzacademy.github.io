Our approach

How our tutors teach

Great results come from great habits. Every AMFAHHZ tutor follows the same proven teaching framework.

  • Diagnose firstA short assessment identifies strengths and gaps before lessons begin.
  • Concepts before shortcutsStudents understand the "why" so they can handle any question the examiner sets.
  • Practise like the real examTopical and timed past papers, marked against the official mark scheme.
  • Keep parents in the loopRegular updates on progress, attendance and next steps.
