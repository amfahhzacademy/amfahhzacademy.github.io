FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does home tuition work?

A qualified tutor comes to your home in Islamabad or Rawalpindi at the agreed time, teaches your child one-on-one and sets practice work between sessions. If a home visit is not possible, we arrange online tuition instead.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. Your first demo class is completely free with no obligation to continue. It helps you judge the tutor and teaching style before you decide.

Which exam boards do you cover?

We teach Cambridge (O Level, A Level, IGCSE), Edexcel, GCSE, IB, American curriculum, Pakistani boards (Matric, FSc, ICS, ICom, FA) and entry tests such as SAT, MDCAT, ECAT and NUST NET.

Can I choose the tuition timings?

Absolutely. We schedule tuition around your family's availability, including evenings and weekends. Evening slots are available for home tuition.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the level, subject, number of sessions per week and whether tuition is at home or online. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share a clear fee plan after your free demo.

Do tutors provide solved past papers or notes?

Tutors may use selected past-paper questions and exam-style practice during lessons according to the student's needs. Complete solved past papers and specially prepared notes are not part of standard tuition. Additional preparation or extensive past-paper resources can be discussed separately with the tutor.
