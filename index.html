FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do online classes work?

Classes are held live on Zoom or Google Meet. Your tutor uses a digital whiteboard, shares notes after each session and assigns practice work between classes.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. Your first demo class is completely free with no obligation to continue. It helps you judge the tutor and teaching style before you decide.

Which exam boards do you cover?

We teach Cambridge (O Level, A Level, IGCSE), Edexcel, GCSE, IB, American curriculum, Pakistani boards (Matric, FSc, ICS, ICom, FA) and entry tests such as SAT, MDCAT, ECAT and NUST NET.

Can I choose my class timings?

Absolutely. We schedule classes around your availability, including evenings and weekends, and we work across time zones for students abroad.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the level, subject and number of classes per week. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share a clear fee plan after your free demo.
