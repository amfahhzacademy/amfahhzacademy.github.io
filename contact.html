FAQ

Before you get in touch

What do I need for online classes?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a stable internet connection. A headset and a notebook are recommended. We share the class link before every session.

Do you teach students outside Pakistan?

Yes. We are fully remote and teach students across the Middle East, UK, Europe, North America and Asia, scheduling classes in your local time zone.

How quickly can classes start?

In most cases we can arrange your free demo class within one to two days of your enquiry.

Can I change my tutor?

Of course. If you feel another teaching style would suit your child better, let us know and we will arrange a new match.
